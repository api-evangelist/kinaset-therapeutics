# Kinaset Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Medfield, Massachusetts developing novel inhaled
> therapeutics for severe respiratory disease. Lead candidate frevecitinib (KN-002) is a dry-powder
> inhaled pan-JAK inhibitor (JAK1/JAK2/JAK3/TYK2) positioned as a non-invasive anti-inflammatory
> add-on to standard of care for all severe asthma patients, regardless of the underlying cause of
> inflammation, and explored in COPD.

## Clinical programs

- frevecitinib (KN-002) — inhaled dry-powder pan-JAK inhibitor for severe asthma; also evaluated in COPD.
- Phase 1/1b: [NCT05006521](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05006521) — 49 healthy volunteers plus 55 subjects with mild or moderate-to-severe asthma.
- Phase 2b PANAIRAMA: [NCT07532265](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07532265) — first patient dosed July 2026.
- FDA IND clearance for frevecitinib in asthma announced ahead of the Phase 2b program.
